1.  **[SEVERITY: critical]** **Broken message bubble alignment (Visual Rhythm failure).** The first message (green bubble, top-right) is aligned to the far right edge, but subsequent messages (gray bubbles, left) and the final green bubble (bottom-right) do not share a consistent max-width or margin. The layout feels like messages are "floating" without a clear grid column for left vs. right alignment, breaking the conversational flow rhythm.
2.  **[SEVERITY: moderate]** **Inconsistent message bubble padding.** The green bubbles (sent messages) appear to have tighter internal padding (especially horizontal) compared to the gray bubbles (received messages). This makes the sent messages look "cramped" next to the more generous received messages.
3.  **[SEVERITY: moderate]** **Timestamp positioning inconsistency.** In the green bubbles, timestamps are right-aligned inside the bubble. In the gray bubbles, timestamps are left-aligned below the text. While this is a common pattern, the vertical distance between the text and timestamp differs between the two bubble types, creating uneven vertical rhythm down the thread.
4.  **[SEVERITY: minor]** **Input area composition.** The "Write a message..." input field and the send button (paper plane icon) are well-spaced, but the helper text "Enter to send - Shift+Enter for a new line" is very close to the bottom edge of the container, risking a cramped footer feel on smaller screens.
5.  **[SEVERITY: minor]** **Header action button sizing.** The "Members" and "Delete room" buttons in the top-right header appear to have slightly different internal padding or font sizes compared to the "New room" button seen on the previous page (Image 2), breaking cross-page component consistency.
